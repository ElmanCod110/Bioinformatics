Using this threshold, the control differs from each treatment, and B100 differs from B200 and from the four groups with zero recorded growth. The difference between B200 and the zero-growth groups is below the HSD threshold. The four zero-growth groups have identical recorded values.

Kruskal-Wallis check
A Kruskal-Wallis test on the same seven groups gives H = 18.5042 and p = 0.0050884. This is a non-parametric comparison of the group distributions.

Notes and limitations
There are three recorded observations per group. Several groups contain only zero values, and the residual normality assumption for ordinary ANOVA is not well supported by these data. The ANOVA result should therefore be read with this limitation in mind; the Kruskal-Wallis result is included as a supplementary check, not as a replacement for checking the experimental design.

The labels A and B are retained exactly as supplied. Their meanings were not defined in the data provided.

The measurements are recorded in cm. The table does not state whether these values are total colony diameters or growth values after subtracting the initial inoculum plug. No inhibition percentages are calculated here for that reason.

The raw values are preserved as supplied. B200 is recorded as 0.10, 0.00 and 0.00 cm.
