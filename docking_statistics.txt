At alpha = 0.05, the row effect is not statistically significant (p = 0.225900903), while the column effect is statistically significant in this model (p = 0.033087615).

The row and column labels in the supplied Excel output correspond to compounds and protein targets, respectively. This is an exploratory analysis only.

Important limitation
There is one docking score for each compound-target pair. There are no independent replicate observations in this 4 by 4 matrix. The two-factor ANOVA without replication cannot estimate a separate compound-by-target interaction, and its error term does not represent independent experimental replication. Its p-values should not be treated as strong confirmatory evidence. The score matrix is primarily useful for descriptive comparison and ranking.

The docking scores are computational estimates and do not by themselves demonstrate enzyme inhibition or biological activity.
